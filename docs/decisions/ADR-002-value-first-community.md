# ADR-002: Value-First Community Over Hard In-Course Upsells

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Source:** Alex Call 02  

## Context

Alex spent heavily on leads while recording the course and pitched Inner Circle in essentially every video. That maximized short-term cash but failed to build a cult/community.

## Decision

Near-term monetization priority is: get people into the course, deliver an exceptional experience, and **avoid hard upsells**. Recurring private community (~$100–200/mo) should be something members **ask to join**, not something hammered in every lesson.

## Consequences

- Course content and Agent OS messaging are rewritten away from constant Inner Circle CTAs.
- Revenue may dip short-term; community MRR and trust become the compensating assets.
- Affiliates are allowed only when they do not recreate upsell fatigue.
