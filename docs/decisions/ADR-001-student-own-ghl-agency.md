# ADR-001: Students Own Their Own GHL Agency Accounts

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-08-08  
**Source:** Alex Call 02  

## Context

Students buy SSA training to run AI agencies for *their* clients. GHL agency plans differ: ~$97 (3 sub-accounts), ~$297 (unlimited), ~$497 (unlimited + AI). Agents and humans often default to “put the student in our sub-account.”

## Decision

Every student must purchase and operate **their own GHL agency account**. SSA must not house students as sub-accounts if they need to onboard their own clients.

## Consequences

- Onboarding docs and Agent OS prompts must forbid SSA-sub-account tenancy for students.
- Support burden shifts to helping students pick the right GHL tier.
- Affiliate/education around GHL billing becomes part of fulfillment.
